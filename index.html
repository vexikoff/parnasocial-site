Why Parnasocial?

Parnasocial combines a clean interface with a wide range of features that let you make your media experience your own.

Browse your active media directory, easily move tracks into your playlist, and keep everything organized in one place.
Customize your experience with themes, audio settings, playback options, hardware acceleration and more.

From crossfade and Bass Boost to Discord Rich Presence and custom CSS, Parnasocial gives you control without overwhelming you with complexity.
